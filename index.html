Our Mission
To make accessing quality healthcare in Ghana as convenient, trustworthy and seamless as it is in leading international healthcare systems.
Why GhanaCare Concierge

Healthcare in Ghana has advanced. Navigating it shouldn't be hard.

Ghana continues to see growth in diaspora returnees, business travel, tourism, diplomatic missions and expatriate assignments. Yet many visitors face common challenges:

  • Difficulty identifying trusted healthcare providers
  • Long waiting times at hospitals
  • Transportation challenges when ill
  • Limited understanding of Ghana's healthcare system
  • Lack of continuity with existing providers abroad
  • Difficulty obtaining prescriptions and laboratory services
  • Need for discreet and private medical care
  • Elderly family members requiring home-based attention

Rather than operating as a traditional clinic, GhanaCare Concierge is a healthcare coordination and concierge platform connecting you with trusted physicians, nurses, laboratories, pharmacies, specialists, ambulance providers and hospitals.
